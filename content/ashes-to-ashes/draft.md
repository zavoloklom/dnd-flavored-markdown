# Ashes to Ashes

*A gothic mystery adventure for levels 2–3*

https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wa/frozen-sick

---

## Adventure Summary

A creeping unease haunts the outskirts of a small farming town. Locals whisper of strange lights and shambling shapes among the graves of the old cemetery, yet no one has been harmed. The mayor asks the adventurers to investigate and to return an urn of ash to a ruined crypt in the Palesmoke Hills. But the restless dead are not what they seem — they guard against something far worse.

**Themes:** gothic mystery, redemption, moral choice, quiet horror.
**Recommended Length:** 2–3 sessions of 3–4 hours each.

---

## Adventure Hooks

* **A Favor Owed.** The party helped a local priest or official once before; now that person calls on them to deal with the growing unrest.
* **Unfinished Rites.** A friend or mentor of the party died recently, and their ashes are among those to be buried.
* **A Hunter's Trail.** One of the heroes has hunted infernal creatures before; rumors of a hell hound nearby catch their attention.

---

## Act I: Shadows Over the Graveyard

**Location:** The farming town of Brindlefield.

The party arrives to find fearful but unharmed villagers. The cemetery keeper swears that undead have been seen walking among the tombs but doing no harm. The mayor offers a modest reward (50 gp) to any who will carry an urn of ash to the family crypt of House Maren, two miles north in the Palesmoke Hills.

**Investigation:**

* **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** reveals the mayor is hiding guilt; his family desecrated graves years ago to fund the town's expansion.
* **DC 13 Intelligence (Religion)** identifies the urn's sigils as druidic rather than clerical.
* **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion)** with the cemetery keeper yields that the undead bow their heads before a certain ruined statue each night.

---

## Act II: The Druid's Shack

Following the road through overgrown fields, the heroes find a **half-elf druid**, *Seris of the Thicket*, wounded and beset by **three wolves** and a **hell hound**. The wolves fight fiercely; the hound retreats if reduced below half hit points, vanishing into smoke.

**Roleplay with Seris:**
Seris believes the undead are guardians bound to protect the region from infernal forces. She begs the party not to harm them and warns that the ashes in the urn belong to her mentor, whose magic holds a barrier against demonic intrusion.

* **DC 14 Wisdom (Medicine)** stabilizes her.
* **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** convinces her to share a druidic token (a charm that pacifies undead if held visibly).

---

## Act III: The Ashes Hills

Ash-colored grass and curling mists blanket the hills. Undead wander silently: **2d4 skeletons** and **1d4 zombies**, moving with eerie purpose. They do not attack unless the party strikes first or includes a tiefling, whom they perceive as infernally tainted.

**Exploration Elements:**

* **Grave Signs.** The undead place stones in spiral patterns (DC 12 Religion reveals they mark wards of protection).
* **Wailing Wind.** The cry of the hell hound echoes through the hills. A DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check reveals it always circles the crypt but never enters.

**Optional Encounter:** If the party lingers or attacks the undead, the hell hound reappears, harrying them toward the crypt.

---

## Act IV: The Maren Crypt

The crypt door is sealed with druidic wards. Inside, cold blue light emanates from a **skeleton clutching a glowing staff**. The staff pulses like a heartbeat. The moment the party approaches, all nearby undead stir and advance defensively.

**Encounters:**

* **The Guardian:** 1 *skeleton champion* (use *skeleton* with +2 AC, +10 hp, multiattack) defends the staff.
* If the hell hound survived, it bursts in mid-battle, attacking indiscriminately. The undead then shift focus to destroy it.

**Resolving the Mystery:**

* The staff sustains a ward that seals a demonic rift below the crypt. Removing or breaking it releases infernal energy.
* **DC 15 Arcana or Religion** check identifies the nature of the staff.
* **Returning the Ashes** to the crypt and touching them to the staff calms the undead and restores the seal.
* Destroying the staff frees the undead but weakens the ward, inviting future demonic incursions.

**Moral Choice:**

* **Preserve the ward** (peace but eternal unrest for the dead).
* **Release the spirits** (mercy but future danger).

---

## Epilogue

* If the ward remains intact, the undead slowly crumble to dust; the town remains safe but haunted by guilt.
* If destroyed, demonic corruption seeps subtly into the hills — perfect for a follow-up adventure.

Seris may become an ally or tragic victim, depending on the heroes' decisions.

*Ashes to Ashes* offers quiet horror and moral depth over raw combat. It rewards empathy and observation as much as swordplay.
