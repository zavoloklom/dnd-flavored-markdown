## Page: Inside Cover

Example:

```md
::: page {type="inside-cover"}

![Cover](./image-url) {class="cover"}

# Monk

A Martial Artist of Supernatural Focus
```

- Special class "cover" would convert image into full-page background
- H1 Heading would be positioning at the bottom of the page with specific style
- Next line after H1 would be transformed into tagline meaning - background and specific style
- Footnote and page-number are hidden here
